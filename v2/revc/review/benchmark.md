# Benchmark: owner's folding aluminium event stair vs off-the-shelf systems

Date: 2026-09-26. Web research only.

**Method and verification caveat.** The network proxy blocked direct page and PDF fetches for every manufacturer site (layher, peri, haki, prolyte, eurotruss, litectruss, alustage, nivtec, scenesalg). Every figure below therefore comes from **search-engine extracts of the cited pages**, not from reading the documents directly. Tags used:

- **[S]**: the figure appeared in a search extract attributed to the cited URL. It is likely correct, but check it against the datasheet before relying on it.
- **[U]**: unverified or inferred, including my own arithmetic and conversions.
- **"n/f"**: not found in public sources.

No prices or ratings have been invented.

## 1. System-by-system findings

### 1.1 Layher Allround stairway tower 750 (public / event access)
- **Use:** Layher positions it for public areas and for event stage and grandstand access "due to its reduced rise dimensions" [S] ([baumagazin-online](https://www.baumagazin-online.de/d/neue-systemloesungen-fuer-layher-treppentuerme/), [Layher NA](https://layherna.com/public-access-stairs/)).
- **Material:** steel U-stringers ("Treppenwange 750 LW") with standard Allround steel or aluminium decks as treads. The flight width varies with the decks: 1.29 to 2.07 m [S] ([Layher NA](https://layherna.com/public-access-stairs/)).
- **Geometry:** rise 166 mm, going 310 to 315 mm (about 28°) [S] ([hawego 8-step stringer](https://www.hawego.de/layher-u-treppenwange-750-lw-8-stufen-lg-2639.008)).
- **Load:** 7.5 kN/m² at a 2.07 m flight width [S] (same source). Layher's stair range as a whole covers 2 to 7.6 kN/m² [S].
- **Weights and price:**
  - Stringer 750 LW, 8 steps (1.50 m lift, 2.57 m run): **36.4 kg each, €509 incl. VAT** [S] ([hawego](https://www.hawego.de/layher-u-treppenwange-750-lw-8-stufen-lg-2639.008)).
  - Stair railing 750 T12, 5 steps: 24.3 kg [S] ([hawego](https://www.hawego.de/layher-treppengelaender-750-t12-5-stufen-lg-2616.105)).
  - Guardrail T12 "mit Kindersicherung" (child-safe infill) 2.57 m: 35.8 kg, 1.10 m high, and height-adjustable [S] ([hawego](https://www.hawego.de/layher-gelaender-t12-mit-kindersicherung-2-57-m-lg-2616.257), [pasvatek](https://www.pasvatek.de/produkt/layher-ev-gelaender-207m-x-110m-t12-mit-kindersicherung-sicherheitsgelaender-hoehenverstellbar/)).
  - The 12 cm bar spacing on T12 appeared only in a generic extract [U].
- **Connections:**
  - Wedge-head into rosette; ledgers have an AutoLock (gravity pre-lock) [S] ([Layher NA Allround](https://layherna.com/allround-scaffolding/)).
  - The handrail uses only three parts: handrail holder, joint and tube [S] ([Layher NA](https://layherna.com/public-access-stairs/)).
  - Deck locking clamps [S].
  - Height adjustment comes from Allround base jacks (standard scaffold) [U: range not captured].
- **Standards:** German approval (Z-8.1-series) and EN 12811 context are probable but **not captured** [U]. The UK distributor claims Building Regulations Part K/B compliance [S] ([georgeroberts](https://georgeroberts.com/products/system-scaffolding/layher-500750-public-access-staircase/)).
- **Folding:** none. The stair is erected from components.

### Observations
- **Load rating:** the owner's 7.5 kN/m² matches the public-access leaders (Layher 750, PERI Public, HAKI PAS, Nivtec). Typical stage stairs (Prolyte about 4.9 kN/m², Alustage and Eurotruss unpublished) are below it.
- **Barrier rating:** the owner's 3.0 kN/m target is far above what the stage-stair makers publish; Nivtec gives about 1.0 kN/m. None of the scaffold makers' barrier kN/m figures could be captured.
- **Stair pitch:** the owner's **35° (175/250) is steeper** than every public-access system found: HAKI 31° at 167/280, Layher 750 about 28° at 166/310. The 2R+G check gives 600 mm, which is within the usual 590–650 comfort window [U: own arithmetic]. Even so, the 250 mm going is at the short end for crowd stairs and is the most likely point a reviewer would challenge. Check the national or venue rules that apply (e.g. Part K public stair, DIN 18065).
- **Weight:** the owner's 88–98 kg is the same class as LITEC (98.5 kg for 7 steps) and Alustage SMA (88.15 kg). This suggests the target is realistic for a folding aluminium unit. It is heavy for two-person handling, though; both benchmarks are the same.
- **Certification:** only Nivtec (TÜV to EN 13814) publicly claims a type test among the event-stair products found. The scaffold systems rely on national approvals and building-code conformity. No EN 1090-3 claims were found (n/f).

## 3. Ideas worth borrowing

1. **Gravity pre-lock plus a positive secondary lock** (PERI gravity lock, Layher AutoLock). A pin or wedge drops home under its own weight on assembly, so a hooked connection holds before the crew taps it tight. Apply this to the hook-on landing connection and to the barrier post sockets.
2. **Tamper-proof, child-proof locks on barrier parts** (HAKI PAS). Use a lock that the public cannot release by hand and that needs a tool or a two-motion action. It is worth adding where posts or infill panels are removable.
3. **Drop-over barrier posts on fixed bolts or spigots** (Nivtec: the "railing pulls down over the railing bolts"). This gives fast, solid, gravity-seated posts with no loose pins. Add a captive snap pin against uplift.
4. **Separate child-safe infill as an add-on panel** (Layher T12 "mit Kindersicherung", HAKI's 100 mm sphere rule). Keep the base barrier frame and add clip-in mesh or vertical-bar panels for public or viewing use. Also meet the **100 mm sphere rule between treads (open risers)**, not only in the rails.
5. **Handrail kit with very few parts** (Layher's holder + joint + tube). Standardising one holder that fits any post reduces loose parts and errors.
6. **Spring-assisted folding** (LITEC). A gas strut or torsion spring counterbalances the flight during unfolding. This matters at about 95 kg and reduces pinch and drop hazards.
7. **Self-adjusting pitch / self-levelling treads** (LITEC, Eurotruss, AdjustaStairs). Parallelogram treads keep steps level across a height range. This could widen the unit's usable height band beyond what screw jacks alone give. Note the trade-off: the rise/going and the rating must hold across the whole range.
8. **Drained anti-slip aluminium treads** (LITEC drainage holes; HAKI chequer plate). Specify slip resistance (e.g. an R-class or pendulum value) and drainage in the design basis.

Further points to consider:
- Aim for an **EN 13814 / TÜV type test**, as Nivtec has. It is the recognised route for event structures.
- Publish a **single-sheet rating table** (kN/m², point load, barrier kN/m, height range), as the scaffold makers do.
